    How SLOW works

    Wrap
    Your token becomes a SLOW position with a timelock you pick.
    Reverse
    Until the lock expires, you can pull it back. Wrong address, changed mind, stolen key.
    Claim
    After it expires, the recipient takes the underlying. A keeper can do it for them if you tip.
    Recover
    If nobody ever claims, you can take it back 30 days after expiry.
    Guardian
    Optional cosigner who must approve every outflow from your account.

    Every transfer is an ERC-1155 position that draws its own picture on chain.
